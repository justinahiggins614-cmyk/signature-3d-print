🖨 Print guide From download to object — any printer, any material

1 · PICK A TANGIBLE

Search or browse the vault, open any item. Every one of the 560,000+ records has its own printable medallion, generated fresh from its ID.

2 · DOWNLOAD A UNIVERSAL FILE

STL (every slicer reads it), OBJ (universal backup), or 3MF (modern universal). The mesh is generated in your browser — nothing to wait for, no account, no cost.

3 · SLICE FOR YOUR PRINTER

There is no universal G-code — G-code is baked for one printer model. Slice the STL in your own slicer with the profile below for your machine, then print.

4 · HOLD IT

Medallion: 50 mm across, ~7 mm tall — a desk piece. Keychain variant: same medallion with a fused loop. Display it, gift it, collect them.

⚙ Slicer profiles Copy into your slicer — tuned per printer class

Honest note: these profiles are starting points from the medallion's geometry (flat base, no overhangs beyond the rim, fused shells). Your exact temperatures depend on your filament brand — run a temperature tower if you want perfection.
